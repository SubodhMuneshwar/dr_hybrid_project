Clinical Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.4 Clinical Baseline
1. Data Collected 2. How Data is Used 3. Security Standards 4. Retention & Storage 5. Patient Rights 6. Healthcare Compliance
1

Information We Process

RetinaScan AI collects only the minimal data points necessary to perform machine learning classification and generate diagnostic interpretability heatmaps:

  • Retinal Fundus Images: Standard PNG or JPEG ocular fundus scans submitted for deep learning severity grading.
  • Patient Pseudonyms: De-identified patient identifiers or names assigned to track chronological scan progressions.
  • Scan Metadata: Timestamps, confidence scores, probability vectors, and Grad-CAM attention coordinates.
  • Diagnostic Logs: System operational logs and inference latencies used solely for operational maintenance.
2

How Information Is Used

Submitted data is exclusively utilized for diagnostic inference and longitudinal patient record keeping:

  • Executing neural inference to determine diabetic retinopathy severity classification (Classes 0 through 4).
  • Generating Class Activation Maps (Grad-CAM) to highlight pathological vascular patterns and exudates.
  • Exporting clinical diagnostic PDF summary reports for ophthalmology consultations.
  • Maintaining sequential scan histories within the local deployment database.
  • We never sell, monetize, or transmit medical images to commercial advertising brokers.
3

Data Security Architecture

The platform implements defense-in-depth architectural safeguards to protect medical records against unauthorized access, tampering, or exfiltration:

Input Sanitization

Strict regex validation, filename sanitization, and path-traversal prevention guards.

Atomic Storage

Thread-safe atomic writes and OS file-locking to prevent concurrent record corruption.

CSRF Protection

Cryptographic session verification tokens on all form endpoints and image uploads.

4

Data Retention & Local Processing

All retinal images and patient histories remain on the deployment environment specified by the deploying clinic or researcher. Unless configured otherwise, image processing takes place entirely within local memory during the inference cycle.

5

User and Patient Rights

In accordance with international privacy principles (including GDPR and regional health privacy statutes), individuals whose data is processed hold the right to:

  • Right to Access: Review diagnostic history and confidence ratings recorded under a patient profile.
  • Right to Portability: Export structured patient records and consultation PDFs.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent removal of uploaded scans and derived attention maps.
6

HIPAA & Regulatory Disclosures

This installation of RetinaScan AI serves as an investigational research demonstration. For commercial or hospital-wide deployment involving Protected Health Information (PHI), organizations must deploy behind a certified Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with hardened access-control policies.
