Standardized 5-Level ICDR Grading

Implemented per the International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy Disease Severity Scale

Class 0 Low Risk

No Diabetic Retinopathy

No evident vascular abnormalities, microaneurysms, or retinal hemorrhages detected on fundus examination.

Class 1 Low-Moderate Risk

Mild Non-Proliferative DR

Presence of isolated microaneurysms only. Indicates early structural capillary changes requiring annual surveillance.

Class 2 Moderate Risk

Moderate NPDR

More than microaneurysms, with hard exudates, cotton-wool spots, and venous dilation across quadrants.

Class 3 High Risk

Severe NPDR

Follows the 4-2-1 rule: >20 intraretinal hemorrhages in 4 quadrants, venous beading in 2, or IRMA in 1.

Class 4 Critical High Risk

Proliferative DR (PDR)

Neovascularization on the optic disc (NVD) or elsewhere (NVE), preretinal hemorrhages, and elevated risk of tractional retinal detachment. Urgent ophthalmological intervention required.
