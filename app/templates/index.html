Clinical Context

The Screening Bottleneck

Over 400 million adults live with diabetes globally. Timely detection prevents up to 95% of vision loss, yet screening systems face severe challenges.

Manual Assessment Gaps

  • Diagnostic Fatigue: Reviewing hundreds of fundus images daily increases human error and micro-aneurysm oversight.
  • Observer Variability: Differences in specialist grading thresholds lead to inconsistent stage categorization.
  • Backlog Delays: Days or weeks between fundus capture and specialist review risk irreversible progression.

RetinaScan AI Precision

  • Consistent Triage: 24/7 reproducible, standardized classification across all 5 clinical stages.
  • Hybrid Feature Fusion: Combines deep neural representations with classical texture micro-descriptors.
  • Instant Explainability: Real-time Grad-CAM localization maps highlighting areas of interest immediately.
Architecture

Why Hybrid Features?

Deep learning excels at high-level abstractions, while texture descriptors capture fine vascular changes. Our architecture fuses both for enhanced diagnostic sensitivity.

Deep Embeddings

Pretrained ImageNet backbones (VGG16 with Global Average Pooling or DenseNet121) extract rich structural feature representations.

512 Deep Vectors

LBP Descriptors

Uniform Local Binary Patterns (radius 3, 24 points) evaluate rotational micro-texture irregularities across retinal vessels.

26 Histogram Bins

GLCM Haralick

Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix measures contrast, dissimilarity, homogeneity, energy, correlation, and angular second moment.

24 Spatial Angles
