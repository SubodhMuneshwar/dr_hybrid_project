Why Early Detection Matters

Over 80% of vision loss from Diabetic Retinopathy can be prevented through timely screening and early intervention.

Yet screening systems face critical challenges:

  • 👤 Diagnostic Fatigue: Manual review of hundreds of fundus images daily increases human error
  • 📊 Observer Variability: Inconsistent specialist grading leads to missed diagnoses
  • ⏱️ Screening Delays: Days or weeks between capture and review risk irreversible progression

RetinaScan AI solves this: 24/7 reproducible, standardized classification with instant Grad-CAM localization.
